Rip currents

If you feel yourself being pulled away from shore, stay calm and avoid fighting the current directly. Swim parallel to the beach until you’re out of the fast-moving water, then swim back in.

Cold water shock

Cold water can trigger sudden gasping and rapid breathing. Enter slowly, control your breathing, and use a wetsuit when appropriate.

Tide awareness

Outgoing tides can strengthen currents around piers, rocks, and narrow channels. Plan entry/exit points and avoid swimming alone in unfamiliar areas.

Before you swim

Check flags, swell, wind direction, and visibility. Tell someone your plan and swim within your limits.
